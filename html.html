Frequently Asked Questions
What is Sunnyside?
∧
Sunnyside is your go-to destination in Illinois for adult-use and medical cannabis, cannabis education, and support. We're more than just a dispensary — we're here to help you explore cannabis comfortably, confidently, and judgment-free.
Who can shop at Sunnyside in Illinois?
∧
Anyone 21 or older with a valid government-issued ID can purchase adult-use cannabis in Illinois. No medical card is required for adult-use customers.
Do you serve both medical and adult-use customers?
∧
Yes! Many of our Illinois locations serve both medical and adult-use customers, with separate menus and dedicated service areas for each to ensure a smooth experience for all shoppers.
What cannabis products are available at Sunnyside IL locations?
∨
We offer a wide range of premium adult-use cannabis products, including:
  • Flower
  • Vapes and cartridges
  • Edibles
  • Pre-rolls
  • Tinctures and topicals
  • Accessories
All from trusted brands known for quality and consistency.
Can someone help me find the right product?
∨
Definitely. Our team of friendly Wellness Advisors is here to guide you — whether you're new to cannabis or looking for something specific. We're committed to helping you make informed choices in a relaxed, no-pressure environment.
Can I order online and pick up in-store?
∨
Yes! You can browse menus and place orders online for in-store pickup at all Sunnyside IL locations. It's a quick and easy way to secure your favorite products.
Are there daily deals or specials?
∨
Yes — we offer daily savings, product spotlights, and exclusive promotions that rotate regularly. Check your local store page or visit in-store to see what's available.
What makes Sunnyside different from other dispensaries?
∨
Sunnyside stands out for our educational approach, friendly service, and wide product selection. We create a welcoming space for all customers — whether you're a first-timer or a regular — and we're always happy to help you explore new options.
Where are Sunnyside dispensaries located in Illinois?
∨
We have several locations across Illinois, including Chicago, Schaumburg, Rockford, Buffalo Grove, and more. Use our store locator online to find the closest Sunnyside and see what's in stock near you.
